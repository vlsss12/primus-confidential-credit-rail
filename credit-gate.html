Until a real DVC proof is produced and independently verified, this output is only a schema example—not a receipt or authorization token.

No wallet, Binance account, API key, raw financial data, or attestation is requested or transmitted by this policy compiler. SHA-256 identifies the policy configuration; it is not itself a zero-knowledge proof.
